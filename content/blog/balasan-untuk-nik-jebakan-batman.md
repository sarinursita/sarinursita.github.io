## Back to your Jebakan Batman xD

Ur notes are spot on, Nik! Aku jadi nyadar problem framing itu penting banget. Each problem has to be identified whether its a problem you can solve with AI. Ur example prediksi occupancy rate itu REAL, I can see how people in this industry will find it relatable.

My thoughts tentang AI for property valuation, is that DATA is again the important factor here. Kamu tau kan valuers itu awal karirnya ngapain? Yes, keliling SCBD or wherever it is to get data pembanding. Data penjualan rumah setipe di sekitar dlm bbrp waktu terakhir. Pura2 jadi pembeli buat nanya2 harga. Nyari iklan rumah dijual. Telponin tiap pemilik rumah yang pasang spanduk dijual di pagar rumah. Etc etc etc. Itu hard work yang NO SANE VALUERS WILL GIVE IT FOR FREE. They keep the data for themselves. Itu kalo company sampe gak capture datanya ya WASSALAM, begitu valuer resign ya udh itu data kebawa semua sama orang tsb.

And that kind of data is what is needed for a property valuation company to build an AI valuer. I'm kinda skeptical for the data availability, but I honestly don't know since I'm not a valuer and doesn't follow the industry news. Who knows, sekarang pemerintah keep track data penjualan property secara komprehensif baik yang offer price maupun transaction price and they can query that data for free or for a fee (I knooow, that sounds too good to be true lol).

BTW!! Your notes are my life saver when I took the pretest in my class. Data imbalance and dimension exploding is what stick to me the most. Blackbox problem - ur example is always a pleasure to read I can just imagine ur client gebrak meja krn dikasih nilai valuasi di bawah harga yang mereka ekspek xD
