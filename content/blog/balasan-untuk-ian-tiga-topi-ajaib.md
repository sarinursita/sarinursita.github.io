Hi Ian, it's interesting that you bring up topi desainer tentang keputusan strategis sebelum ngadain kelas. It's kinda instinctive for me at this point. Tujuan dan hasil proyek Cerivitas biasanya aku taro di poster/deskripsi proyek. Kenapa materi A sebelum materi B is based on my mastery of the subject, like kenapa harus bisa nulis cerpen dulu sebelum nulis novel. Kenapa harus belajar outlining and story structure dulu sebelum editing.

That part when you said seberapa besar kapasitas kognitif audiens sebelum otak mereka ngebul makes me laugh. Yeah it's especially hard to make a project for kids with big age range cause there are some outliers that can survive or even thrive in the class. Ada anak 7 tahun yang udah bisa nulis dan ngikutin proyek menulis Cerivitas, but it doesn't mean every 7yo CAN follow or SHOULD join the class.

Itu sebabnya aku bilang sebenernya usia bukan hambatan atau faktor penentu yang strict karena experience tiap orang beda, dan aku minta ortu dampingi buat anak2 yg daftar sementara mereka di luar recomended age range. Unfortunately, nggak semua ortu juga tahu apa bentuk pendampingan yang perlu mereka berikan ke anaknya yang ikut kelas. Dan aku juga salah karena nggak memberikan panduan untuk para ortu ini.

Topi Kuli/Developer - "bikin grup WhatsApp biar infonya gak ketumpuk stiker komedi bapak-bapak" wkwkw.. u wont believe what happened in Cerivitas group class, tiap opening itu anak2 saling chat sampe 1000+ message and thats why aku bilang ke ortu yg join grup that they might want to set the grup to mute during that time xD but alas, grup kan dibikin buat ngasih info penting so theoretically they should tune in there.

Thats why I think a proper messaging platform kyk Discord might be better than WA, karena aku bisa bikin channel Important Announcement yang ga boleh di skip dan channel offtopic yang bisa host 9999+ messages. Cuma ya gituuu.. orang2 indo udh kebiasaan pake WA dan susah ganti platformnya (selain discord aku pernah pake fb dan wordpress buat platform diskusi juga lho). WA is still the most effective platform krn emang mereka buka tiap hari.

That checkpoint & postmortem is a part I also often fail Ian. At the end of the project I'm often burn out from the prep and the exec and I just want to finish things nicely. Sometimes (okay, often) I'm also afraid to find out that I failed the kids, so the feedback form is really like a formality or nice thing to have. But as an educator I need to own the responsibility of making sure they learn something and hopefully get what they paid for.

Aku jadi inget ada pretest dan posttest di salah satu matkul S2 aku sekarang, and now I understand the reason why they exists. A proper learning should capture this data. Thanks for reminding me!
